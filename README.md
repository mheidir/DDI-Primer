# DDI Primer
Introduces DNS, DHCP and IPAM technology providing core solutions for critical network infrastructure services from small to large enterprises and telecommunications providers.

## Introduction
This technical handbook is meant to introduce network engineers and specifically DDI consultants to understand the workings of DNS and DHCP services which is important in their line of work and how IP Address Manager (IPAM) is being leveraged to provide a comprehensive visibility, control and action for both services as the Single Source of Truth.

The contents of this book may or may not reflect similar inner workings of the various solutions in the market today and is meant to generalize the idea of why a DDI solution is key for operational teams to comprehend their networks and services using a tool that is able to reduce risk, optimize tasks and simplify the complexity of managing IP address assignments across different infrastructures. Typically the challenge arises from having disparate systems running DNS and DHCP services, capacity and performance levels and gaining up-to-date information about the network that correlates accurately between the data and the network.

As this is an ongoing personal project dependent on free time while juggling work, social and life balance, it will be a marathon to finish this.

The intention is to document my experience as a consultant facing various challenges shared by end users in all fields and industries who are looking for a comprehensive solution to address their immediate need, while at the same time offer the flexibility to extend with additional functional features relevant to their daily tasks.
